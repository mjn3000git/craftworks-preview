Custom Signs

We make and fabricate signs from a variety of wood species, and also from metal, from copper to steel to brass.

Signs can also have integrated LED lighting. There are quite a few options for custom signs, whether for a camp, a dock, a boathouse, or a storefront.

  1. Wood speciesOak, pine, maple, cherry, walnut, cedar
  2. CopperWarm, rich, and develops character with age
  3. SteelClean lines and lasting strength
  4. BrassA classic finish with a nautical heritage
  5. Integrated LEDLighting built into the sign itself
