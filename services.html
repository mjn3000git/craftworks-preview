Services

Property care and handyman services

Serving Queensbury, Glens Falls, and Schroon Lake.

  • Seasonal camp open/close and home watch

    Getting a camp or second home ready for the season and closing it up at the end, plus scheduled check-ins while you are away.

  • Deck, porch, and siding refresh

    Cleaning, repairing, and refreshing decks, porches, and siding that have taken a beating from Adirondack weather.

  • Small carpentry repairs

    Trim, steps, doors, railings, and the other small wood repairs that pile up around a house or camp.

  • Grab bars and railings

    Installing grab bars and railings to make stairs, entries, and bathrooms safer to get around.

  • Haul-away

    Loading up and hauling away debris, old furniture, and clutter from homes, camps, garages, and yards.

  • Fixture swaps

    Swapping out existing fixtures, like-for-like, where no licensed plumbing or electrical work is needed.

Please note: Adirondack Craftworks does not offer licensed plumbing or electrical work. Fixture swaps are limited to work that does not require a licensed plumber or electrician.
