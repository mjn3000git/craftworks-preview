About Adirondack Craftworks

Adirondack Craftworks is a mobile property care and handyman business serving Queensbury, Glens Falls, and Schroon Lake.

We bring the tools and materials to you in our trailer and handle the practical jobs that keep homes and camps in good shape: seasonal opening and closing, home watch, repairs, refreshes, and haul-away.
